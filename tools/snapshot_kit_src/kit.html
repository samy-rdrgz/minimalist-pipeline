Audit

Worked out on opening, from the CSS actually loaded and the components measured below. Each line is an inconsistency to fix or to justify. It's worked out again when the theme or the scale changes.

Foundations

One unit: --unit = 4 px × --scale. Spacing, heights, radii and text come from it; View › Interface scale (a slider, 0.5 to 1.5) resizes everything at once. The only exceptions: hairlines (1 px), the layout's widths, and --h-touch (a fixed 44 px: a finger's target doesn't follow the scale).

Colours: the blue theme

A fixed palette, the blue theme 60 / 30 / 10: 60 % neutral greys, 30 % night-blue tints (top bar, selection), 10 % bright blue -- the "Done" state's. "You" is turquoise, to read on a done diamond. The accents (retake, validated) come from the states' colours; the light theme from the dark one, the same roles.
